## Determinism

- oneshot: 50 of 50 outputs match the first run of their commit byte for byte; 10 of 10 commits matched on every repetition.
- server: 50 of 50 outputs match the first run of their commit byte for byte; 10 of 10 commits matched on every repetition.
- one-shot and server mode produced the same text for 10 of 10 commits.
